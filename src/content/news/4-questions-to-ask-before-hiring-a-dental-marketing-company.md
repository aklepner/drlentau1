When it comes to hiring a dental marketing company, choosing the right one can make a big difference for your practice. You want to ensure that your marketing efforts are in good hands. Here are four essential questions to ask before making your decision.

**1. What Percentage of Your Clients are Dentists?**

It's important to know if the marketing company has experience specifically with dental practices. A company that mainly works with dentists will better understand your needs and have proven strategies that work in the dental field.

**2. Do You Have Long-Term Contracts?**

Some marketing companies require long-term contracts, while others operate on a month-to-month basis. Knowing this upfront will help you understand your commitment and decide if it aligns with your practice's needs.

**3. What are the Monthly Deliverables in Your Marketing Packages?**

Ask about what you'll get each month. Will they handle your social media, create blog posts, or manage Google Ads? Ask for specific deliverables with numbers behind them. Understanding what services are included will help you decide if their package meets your practice’s marketing needs. If you’re curious to see an example of deliverables including pricing check out this page from the DocSites: [https://www.docsites.com/marketing-packages/](https://www.docsites.com/marketing-packages/)

**4. Who Owns the Website Domain?**

This is a crucial question. You want to ensure that your practice owns the website domain. If the marketing company owns it, you might face issues if you decide to change providers in the future.

Selecting the right dental marketing company is an important decision that can significantly impact the success and growth of your dental practice. By asking these four essential questions, you pave the way for informed and confident decision-making.
