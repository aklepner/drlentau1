Speaking topics

Talks I give
most often.

Each talk can run as a keynote, a lecture or a hands-on workshop.

01

Today's Online Marketing Strategies

A complete online marketing plan is one of the best ways to raise new patient numbers and case acceptance. Is your practice using today's digital tools? Have you built a full online presence?

You will

  • See why strong content matters for online ranking
  • Learn how to make your practice stand out online
  • Use social media and online listings to promote your practice
  • Understand why mobile comes first
02

5-Star Strategies

Word of mouth in the digital age is online reviews. Is your practice earning five-star reviews every week? Learn how to improve your reputation, manage your online presence and turn your strengths into new patients.

You will

  • Understand what each part of your online presence does
  • Use the tools available to manage your reputation without extra work
  • Engage patients in ways that earn positive reviews
  • Handle negative feedback so it reflects well on your practice
03

Help With Yelp!

When people search for a new dentist, your reviews show up. What does your Yelp reputation say? Learn the three steps to managing your reputation: get found, stand out and protect yourself from negative reviews.

You will

  • Understand the platform's rules and policies
  • Keep your brand consistent across Yelp and other sites
  • Use Yelp to your advantage
04

Evaluate Your Online Score

In this hands-on course I show you how to grade your own online presence. Is your website holding you back? Is your social media working? We look at the whole picture and find the changes that lead to more revenue.

You will

  • Take control of your online presence
  • Track and manage your online reputation
  • Use every resource available to you
  • Build and keep a consistent online brand
05

OMG! Bad Review! What to Do?!

The perfect practice is not the one with perfect reviews. It is the one with a plan for managing them. A bad review brings up everything from anger to embarrassment. In this 60-minute talk I share how to earn good reviews and handle the bad ones.

You will

  • See why your online reputation matters
  • Learn how the major review sites work
  • Respond to negative feedback the right way
  • Help your team earn positive reviews from patients
