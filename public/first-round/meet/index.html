My story

How I got here.

I followed my father into dentistry. I graduated from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and spent two decades caring for patients in Philadelphia, where Philadelphia Magazine named me one of the city's Top Dentists.

Like most dentists, I built my practice on clinical excellence and word of mouth. Then I noticed the practices growing fastest were not always the best clinically. They were the most visible and the most trusted.

So I got serious about fixing that. I built systems for collecting reviews every day, responding professionally, keeping our listings accurate and staying visible on every platform patients use. The result was 4.9 stars, more than 1,600 reviews, 40+ new patients a month and roughly 85% case acceptance.

Once the system worked, I started teaching it. From the stage at dental meetings across the country. In my books, Raving Patients, 100+ Tips for Getting Five-Star Reviews and now Raving Patients Supercharged. And on the Raving Patients Podcast, nearly 350 episodes in.

Today I serve as General Manager of the dental division at Birdeye, helping practices across the country put the same system in place. Every year I host Supercharge Your Dental Practice, where owners and teams come to learn what is working now.

If you want to be the go-to practice in your area, let's talk. That is fixable.
